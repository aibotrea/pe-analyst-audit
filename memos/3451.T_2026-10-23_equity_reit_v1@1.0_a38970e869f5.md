I applied a one-step downward override for thin balance-sheet headroom (liabilities near 52% of assets against a below-55% convention) and the guided DPU decline from rate hikes. That leaves conviction at 3.

## Key Risks
- Higher-for-longer JPY rates, with BoJ at 1.25% and JGB yields rising. This raises interest costs, which management already guides to a JPY 100 DPU reduction next period, and it pressures cap rates and the yield multiple.
- Balance-sheet headroom: liabilities near 52% of assets against a below-55% convention, plus refinancing risk for a small REIT with limited market depth.
- Small-cap concentration and unverified details. AFFO coverage, top-3 asset concentration and tenant and WALE data could not be confirmed from truncated filings. The new hotel mandate adds cyclicality, and sponsor-dependent acquisitions could dilute NAV.
- Currency-basis noise: beta and the expected market return use a GBP-denominated benchmark. The 5-year IASP.L return is negative at -4.6%, so CAPM alpha is flattered and may be unreliable.
- The distribution figure relies on a secondary news summary, and the DPU estimate (Category B) may differ from issuer-published guidance.

## Invalidation Condition
Exit if the REIT cuts DPU by more than the guided JPY 100 for two consecutive periods, or if AFFO coverage falls below 1.0x. Also exit if the liabilities-to-assets ratio (LTV) rises above 55% or refinancing is completed at materially higher spreads. A sponsor Tosei Corporation withdrawal of pipeline or financial support, or hotel acquisitions that visibly dilute NAV per unit, would also trigger an exit.
