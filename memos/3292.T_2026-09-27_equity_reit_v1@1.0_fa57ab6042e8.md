## Key Risks
- AEON Co., Ltd. credit deterioration or rental renegotiation: the master lease structure means all income is dependent on a single corporate counterparty; any financial stress at the sponsor level directly threatens DPU coverage.
- BoJ rate normalisation acceleration: rising JGB yields compress J-REIT yield spreads and trigger cap-rate expansion, reducing NAV and unit price; AEON REIT's floating-rate debt exposure amplifies this channel.
- Structural retail demand shift: secular e-commerce penetration and changing Japanese consumer behaviour reduce footfall at AEON malls, creating medium-term pressure on AEON Co.'s ability to sustain master lease rents at current levels.
- JPY/GBP currency basis noise in beta and alpha: the IASP.L benchmark is GBP-denominated; all computed beta and alpha values absorb FX co-movement, potentially overstating or understating true property-market sensitivity.
- Thin unit liquidity: average daily volume is low (typically 3,000–10,000 units), creating meaningful market impact risk for position entry or exit, especially at larger position sizes.
