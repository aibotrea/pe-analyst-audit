## Key Risks
- Higher-for-longer SGD interest rates compressing the yield spread versus T-bills and expanding cap rates, which would weigh on both DPU (via higher financing costs) and unit price (via NAV de-rating)
- Global industrial and logistics demand slowdown driven by supply-chain normalisation, tech-sector retrenchment, or US-China trade tensions reducing tenant absorption and rental reversion
- Dilutive equity issuance or rights offering to fund acquisitions, particularly if the Rule 706A acquisition (announced 2026-08-05) is large relative to current market capitalisation
- Capital distribution component of DPU (0.371c of 3.732c in Q2 2026) is a return of capital rather than income, reducing sustainable distributable income slightly versus the headline DPU figure
- IASP.L benchmark currency basis (SGD/GBP) introduces noise into beta and CAPM alpha estimates; backtest calibration is directional only and formal vintage discipline is pending Phase 5
