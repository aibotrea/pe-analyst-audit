## Thesis
Link REIT (0823.HK) is Asia's largest listed REIT by market capitalisation, offering a ~5.95% distribution yield backed by a high-quality, primarily Hong Kong suburban retail and car park portfolio (~97% occupancy) with growing international diversification into Mainland China, Australia, Singapore and the UK. The self-managed structure eliminates manager conflict-of-interest and fee drag, while conservative gearing (~27%) provides significant headroom below the 45% HK REIT Code limit. The OU Monte Carlo simulation yields a 12-month sim return of 7.89% with a PGain of 71.5%, supported by a positive CAPM alpha of 8.63%; however, HK retail concentration and Mainland China consumer softness introduce meaningful downside risk, warranting a one-step qualitative gate reduction to a Moderate (3/5) conviction score. BOCOM International's June 2026 Buy rating with TP HKD 45.70 provides a valuation anchor that supports the base-case re-rating thesis but depends on a stabilisation in Hong Kong's macro environment.

## Key Risks
- Hong Kong retail demand softness and negative rental reversion risk if consumer sentiment deteriorates further, particularly in non-essential retail categories exposed to Mainland tourist flow
- Mainland China portfolio (approximately 13% of NPI) faces persistent structural headwinds from weak consumer confidence and commercial real estate oversupply, which could impair DPU growth
- Interest rate risk: HKD is pegged to USD, so Link REIT's cost of debt is sensitive to US Fed policy; any higher-for-longer rate environment compresses the distribution spread and could widen cap rates
- Beta estimate (0.57 vs IASP.L) absorbs HKD/GBP currency basis noise and may overstate or understate true property market co-movement; CAPM alpha of 8.63% inherits this uncertainty
- Concentration risk: approximately 70% of NPI is derived from Hong Kong assets, making the portfolio highly sensitive to local macro shocks despite ongoing international diversification efforts
