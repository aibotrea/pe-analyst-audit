## Thesis
Comforia Residential REIT offers pure-play exposure to Tokyo urban residential assets managed by Tokyu Fudosan REIT Management, a quality sponsor with a demonstrated pipeline and capital commitment track record. At ¥105,500/unit, the implied distribution yield of ~4.7% provides a meaningful spread over Japan's near-zero policy rate environment, though BoJ normalisation presents an incremental headwind to multiple expansion. The July 2026 equity offering (¥10.3B+) to fund five residential acquisitions is marginally dilutive near-term but accretive to AUM-driven DPU growth; management has already lifted its 2027 profit outlook. A PGain of 72.6% from the OU Monte Carlo and a CAPM alpha of 5.3% versus a weak APAC REIT benchmark support a moderate conviction rating at a 5% maximum position size.
