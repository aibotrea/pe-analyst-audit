The key risk is BoJ rate normalisation compressing J-REIT multiples; the recently authorised JPY 10B unit buyback (August 2026) signals management conviction and provides a near-term technical support catalyst.

## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation: further policy rate increases beyond 0.50% would compress J-REIT NAV multiples and widen required cap rates, potentially eroding 8-15% of unit value independently of NOI performance.
- JPY/GBP and JPY/USD FX volatility: as a JPY-denominated asset benchmarked against GBP IASP.L, beta estimates absorb currency noise; a sharp JPY appreciation could deter foreign capital inflows that support J-REIT valuations.
- Logistics demand cyclicality: concentration in a single sub-sector (logistics/industrial) means any slowdown in e-commerce growth or 3PL over-capacity in Japan could reduce rent reversion upside and weaken occupancy from current near-100% levels.
- BoJ APAC rate data unavailable: get_apac_rates returned empty for Japan as of 2026-09-07, preventing direct confirmation of the current policy rate; the multiple-change assumption is based on inferred normalisation trajectory and is Category C.
- Backtest calibration limitation: Phase 2 calibration is directional only; formal vintage discipline arrives in a later phase. This memo's E(R) components lack audited forward DPU confirmation due to truncated filing bodies.
