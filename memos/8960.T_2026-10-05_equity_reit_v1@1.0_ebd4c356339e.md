## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation: further hikes above 1.25% would widen cap rates, depress NAV, and increase refinancing costs for United Urban's approximately 42% LTV balance sheet
- Hotel/retail asset concentration risk: United Urban's exposure to hospitality and discretionary retail makes income more cyclical than pure office or logistics J-REITs, with occupancy and RevPAR sensitive to domestic consumption and inbound tourism trends
- JPY/GBP currency basis noise in beta estimate (Category B): computed beta of 0.51 absorbs FX co-movement, potentially overstating or understating true property-market correlation with the APAC REIT benchmark
- Distribution coverage compression: rising floating-rate debt costs in a BoJ tightening cycle could erode AFFO coverage below 1.0x if revenues do not keep pace with higher interest expense
- Filing body truncation: the 45th period annual report body (EDINET S100YYJB) was truncated at ~1,500 characters of ~1.2M total; full balance-sheet LTV, WALE, and tenant-concentration data could not be confirmed and are classified Category B estimates
