## Key Risks
- Asset concentration in Singapore prime retail (Orchard Road, Wisma Atria and Ngee Ann City), exposing DPU to tourism and retail-sales cycles and to a small number of anchor leases
- Higher-for-longer rates: a thin spread over the 4.01% T-bill, with refinancing risk as medium-term notes mature at higher coupons, and possible cap-rate widening
- Gearing, AFFO coverage, occupancy and WALE could not be verified from the stored filing bodies, which held only cover pages. The memo relies on news-reported DPU figures
- Beta of 0.25 against IASP.L has only 0.21 correlation and carries currency-basis noise, so CAPM alpha is unreliable. The 5-year benchmark return is negative (-4.9%), which makes the CAPM hurdle unusually low
- Post-divestment income dilution as Japan exposure is removed, if sale proceeds are not redeployed or used to repay debt at an accretive spread

## Invalidation Condition
Exit if Singapore retail occupancy falls below about 95% for two consecutive quarters, if DPU declines more than 5% year-on-year in two successive reporting periods, if AFFO coverage drops below 1.0x, or if aggregate leverage rises above 40% with no clear deleveraging plan. Also exit if the Ebisu Fort proceeds are used for dilutive acquisitions, or if the sponsor (YTL) reduces its support of the REIT, for example through fee or unit-holding changes.
