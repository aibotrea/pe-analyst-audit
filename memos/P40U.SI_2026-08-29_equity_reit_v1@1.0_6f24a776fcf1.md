## Key Risks
- Higher-for-longer Singapore interest rates compressing the yield spread, particularly if MAS maintains a strong SGD policy stance reducing pass-through from rate normalisation.
- Structural retail headwinds: e-commerce penetration and discretionary spending shifts in Singapore could impair tenant renewal rates and rental reversions at Wisma Atria and Ngee Ann City.
- Australian portfolio concentration risk: Myer Centre Adelaide and Plaza Arcade Perth are exposed to AUD/SGD FX translation and local retail market conditions outside core Singapore operations.
- Sponsor quality risk: YTL Corporation is a Malaysian conglomerate with limited Singapore REIT sponsorship track record versus blue-chip local sponsors (CapitaLand, Mapletree); no identified acquisition pipeline to grow AUM.
- Distribution coverage opacity: AFFO coverage data not available in the truncated FY2026 filing body; if payout ratio exceeds cash generation, DPU sustainability risk increases.

## Invalidation Condition
Exit position if: (1) Singapore retail portfolio occupancy falls below 90% for two consecutive half-year reporting periods; or (2) DPU declines by more than 10% year-on-year in any reporting period, signalling deteriorating cash coverage; or (3) MAS-reported aggregate leverage breaches 40% (approaching the 45% regulatory limit), reducing financial flexibility and increasing refinancing risk at current rates; or (4) YTL Corporation formally reduces its unitholding or the manager announces a fee restructuring that shifts incentive alignment away from unitholders.
