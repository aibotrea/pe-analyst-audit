## Thesis
Daiwa Office Investment Corporation (8976.T) is a mid-size Tokyo-focused office J-REIT managed by Daiwa Real Estate Asset Management, providing exposure to Grade-A and Grade-B office properties primarily in central Tokyo wards. The 41st-period semi-annual results (Dec 2025–May 2026) showed rental income of JPY 14,911M, up modestly from JPY 14,758M, indicating stabilising occupancy and incremental rent reversion. The ongoing unit buyback program (up to 7,000 units / JPY 1.5B, July–November 2026) signals management confidence in intrinsic value and provides near-term price support. However, conviction is constrained to Low (2/5) due to the recently confirmed FTSE index exclusion (September 2026), which removes a structural source of passive demand, combined with BoJ rate normalisation headwinds at the 1.0% policy rate and a modest CAPM-implied expected return of ~4.5% against a 4.1% risk-free rate that provides limited compensation for idiosyncratic risk.
