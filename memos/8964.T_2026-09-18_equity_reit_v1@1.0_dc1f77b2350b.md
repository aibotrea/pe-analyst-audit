## Thesis

At JPY 75,000, the estimated distribution yield of ~5.4% provides a meaningful spread over Japan's near-zero policy rate environment, though BoJ rate normalisation introduces a non-trivial refinancing and cap-rate risk that limits conviction. Beta of 0.50 versus IASP.L (GBP-denominated; currency-basis caveat applies) implies moderate correlation with broader APAC REIT benchmark movements. The OU Monte Carlo produces a 12-month sim return of ~4.9% with PGain of 70%, consistent with Moderate conviction, as yield income is partially offset by modest multiple compression risk. The non-discretionary tenant profile (supermarkets, home centres) provides resilience but limits distribution growth to the low-single-digit range, making the investment predominantly a yield-carry story rather than a growth play.
