## Key Risks
- BoJ policy rate normalisation faster than expected, widening financing costs and compressing J-REIT valuations sector-wide via cap rate expansion
- JPY appreciation making the GBP-denominated benchmark (IASP.L) beta less reliable as a co-movement measure, amplifying currency-basis noise in alpha estimates
- Residential rent growth disappointment if Japan's wage growth stagnates, capping DPU upside and keeping distribution yield growth below the 1.5% base assumption
- Concentration in Sekisui House-sourced assets means any deterioration in sponsor pipeline quality or related-party transaction scrutiny by regulators could impair acquisition growth
- Liquidity risk: average daily volume of ~10,000-15,000 units limits position sizing for larger mandates; thin trading could amplify price volatility during sector rotation events
