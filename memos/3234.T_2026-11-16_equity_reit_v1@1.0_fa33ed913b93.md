- Asset and tenant concentration in a small number of Mori Building-developed assets (Roppongi, Toranomon, Atago) and sponsor-linked master-lease arrangements.
- Key fundamentals could not be verified: the filing bodies were truncated before DPU, AFFO coverage, LTV and occupancy tables, so yield and leverage assumptions are unverified, and no news was returned in the search window.

- Not a formal backtest or vintage-disciplined calibration. Macro series other than the T-bill and BoJ rate (FEDFUNDS, T10Y2Y, credit spreads) were not used, so the memo is a directional signal only.
