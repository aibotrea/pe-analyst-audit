## Thesis
M44U is a large Singapore-listed pan-Asian logistics REIT sponsored by Mapletree. It trades near the lower end of its recent range and offers an estimated ~7% distribution yield, well above the 4.1% T-bill. The OU Monte Carlo gives a 12-month simulated return of about 6.5% with a standard deviation of 9.7% and a PGain of about 75%. Beta of 0.38 against IASP.L is low, but the currency-basis caveat applies. Active portfolio recycling (Singapore, China and Australia disposals) is a near-term DPU drag. The yield and coverage inputs are unverified, so conviction is cut one step from 4 to 3.

## Key Risks
- Distribution yield, DPU growth and AFFO coverage are analyst estimates because the retrieved filings gave no financials. Actual figures may differ materially.
- Higher-for-longer rates could pressure refinancing costs and widen cap rates. The 4.1% T-bill leaves a narrower spread than past REIT cycles.
- Dilutive divestments in Singapore, China and Australia may not be redeployed accretively, and China and Australia logistics exposure carries FX and demand risk.
- Beta is low at 0.38 with 0.32 correlation to the GBP-denominated IASP.L. CAPM alpha is flattered by the negative trailing benchmark return and is noisy because of currency basis.
- Backtest-style calibration is a directional signal only, not a formal backtest, because vintage discipline arrives in Phase 5. The independent director retirement on 2026-10-31 slightly reduces audit committee depth.
