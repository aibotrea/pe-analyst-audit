## Key Risks
- Rising BoJ policy rates and JGB yields could widen the required REIT yield, cut valuations and raise refinancing costs. The unit price already fell about 5% over the past month.
- DPU quality: recent results were boosted by a one-off asset sale gain and hotel variable rent that may not recur. I could not verify AFFO or distribution coverage from the truncated filing.
- Office-market and tenant exposure. Concentration, WALE and the lease-expiry profile were not verified, and the hotel segment depends on inbound tourism and the yen.
- Currency and benchmark basis: IASP.L is GBP-denominated, so beta (0.50, correlation 0.38) and alpha are noisy. The negative 5-year benchmark return inflates CAPM alpha, and Rf is a USD rate.
- The Japanese REIT has thin liquidity, with daily volume of roughly 2,000-5,000 units. The unit price level and DPU per unit in press sources were not cross-checked against official disclosures, which adds data risk.
