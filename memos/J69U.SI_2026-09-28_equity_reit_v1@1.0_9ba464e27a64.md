## Thesis

At a trailing distribution yield of approximately 5.82% against a T-bill rate of 4.08%, FCT maintains a meaningful 174bps yield spread, providing income defensiveness even in a higher-for-longer rate environment. The Frasers Property Limited sponsor relationship provides a right of first refusal over a credible pipeline of suburban assets, creating an optionality upside not reflected in base-case DPU growth of 1.5%. With a beta of 0.27 versus IASP.L (currency-basis caveat applies), FCT exhibits materially lower systematic risk than the broader APAC REIT universe, and the OU Monte Carlo PGain of 81.8% and CAPM alpha of 5.06% support an above-average conviction rating.

## Key Risks
- Higher-for-longer SGD interest rates compressing the yield spread and increasing interest expense on refinancing tranches, particularly if gearing rises toward 42-43%.
- Suburban retail footfall softening due to e-commerce substitution or consumer spending slowdown, driving occupancy below 93% and weakening rental reversion assumptions.
- Cap rate expansion driven by global risk-off sentiment or a sustained APAC REIT de-rating, reducing NAV and widening the discount to book.
- Asset disposal risk: news flow indicates Singapore REITs are selling assets that may impact dividends — any FCT disposal at sub-book value or at yields that dilute DPU would be a negative catalyst.
- Sponsor pipeline optionality is not guaranteed; any deterioration in Frasers Property Limited's financial health or strategic reprioritisation could reduce the probability of accretive injections.

## Invalidation Condition
