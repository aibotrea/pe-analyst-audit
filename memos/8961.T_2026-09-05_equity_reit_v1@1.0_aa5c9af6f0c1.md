## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation further compressing the yield spread between distribution yield (~4.8%) and rising Japanese short-term rates, reducing valuation support for the REIT structure.
- Hotel segment revenue volatility: ADR and occupancy are subject to inbound tourism demand swings, yen strength/weakness, and event calendars; a significant demand shock could impair semi-annual DPU.
- Tokyo office market softening: any deterioration in Grade-A vacancy rates or rental reversion below expectations would reduce recurring income and pressure DPU coverage.
- Refinancing risk in a rising rate environment: estimated LTV ~47% means a significant proportion of debt will require refinancing at higher margins, increasing interest cost drag on distributable income.
- Macro data gap: BoJ policy rate relied on FRED proxy (IRSTCI01JPM156N, June 2026 vintage); more recent BoJ decisions are not confirmed in stored macro data, introducing lag risk in the rate assumption.
