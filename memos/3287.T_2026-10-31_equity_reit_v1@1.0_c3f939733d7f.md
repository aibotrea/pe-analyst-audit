## Key Risks
- Hotel and ryokan variable rent is cyclical and depends on tourism and discretionary demand. It is also exposed to yen strength and to shocks to inbound arrivals.
- Rising JGB yields and BoJ tightening could raise financing costs and widen cap rates, compressing NAV and the yield spread. A 4% USD T-bill is a high hurdle against a roughly 4.5% yield.
- Concentration in a single sponsor-operator, with a hospitality-only portfolio and thin liquidity (about 1.5-2.5k units per day), gives high idiosyncratic and exit risk.
- The yield, DPU growth and multiple inputs are Category C, and LTV and AFFO coverage were not verified from the filing. The IASP.L benchmark is in GBP, so beta and alpha carry currency noise.
- Persistent price weakness since August, including a 2026-10-29 sell-off on elevated volume, may reflect information not captured in the stored data. The stored series has a price-adjustment discontinuity on 2026-10-23.
