## Key Risks
- Distribution yield, gearing/LTV and AFFO coverage were not extracted from the truncated filings. E(R) rests on an estimated yield, so the numeric conviction is less reliable than it looks.
- BoJ policy tightening (policy rate 1.25%) and higher JGB yields could widen the yield spread, compress P/DPU and raise refinancing costs.
- Logistics supply wave in the Tokyo and Osaka areas could pressure occupancy and rent reversions, and tenant concentration is typical of single-sponsor J-REITs.
- Currency-basis noise: beta and market return are measured against a GBP benchmark, Rf is a USD T-bill, and the asset is in JPY. Alpha is inflated by the negative 5-year IASP.L return.
- Buyback support is temporary (the JPY1bn programme appears to have completed), and the 12-month OU Monte Carlo is a model output, not a forecast.

## Invalidation Condition
Exit if the next semi-annual results show DPU coverage below 1.0x AFFO or a DPU cut, if occupancy falls materially below 98% for two consecutive reporting periods, if LTV rises above roughly 50%, or if the sponsor stops supplying pipeline assets. Also exit if BoJ tightening lifts J-REIT cap rates enough to push the unit price more than 15% below the current JPY89,300 without a matching NAV decline.
