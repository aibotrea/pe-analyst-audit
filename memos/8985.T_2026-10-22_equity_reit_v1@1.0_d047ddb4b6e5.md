Beta of 0.53 versus IASP.L (currency-basis caveat applies) and 18% volatility point to moderate risk. The 12-month OU Monte Carlo gives an expected return of about 4.9% and a PGain of about 65.6%. The positive CAPM alpha comes largely from the negative trailing benchmark return and should be discounted. The weakest points are an unconfirmed LTV and distribution coverage, and a trading premium to book NAV. Together these support Moderate conviction at a 5% maximum position size.

- The Rf is a USD T-bill rate applied to a JPY asset, and as_of data combines live and stored sources, so this is a directional calibration signal rather than a formal backtest

## Invalidation Condition
Exit if hotel RevPAR or variable-rent DPU declines year on year for two consecutive reporting periods, if LTV rises above 55% or DPU coverage falls below 1.0x, or if the BoJ policy rate rises materially above current levels with Japanese cap rates expanding by 25bps or more. Exit also if the sponsor reduces its pipeline support.
