## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation risk: rising Japanese policy rates compress J-REIT yield spreads versus JGBs, directly impressing cap-rate expansion and unit price depreciation for leveraged residential REITs.
- Sponsor transition uncertainty: the rebranding from Takara Leben Infrastructure Fund to MIRARTH not動産投資法人 reflects a strategic pivot; incomplete pipeline commitments and evolving fee structures may not align with unitholder interests.
- Elevated LTV (~52% estimated): leverage near the J-REIT convention ceiling limits financial flexibility for accretive acquisitions and increases refinancing risk in a rising-rate environment.
- Filing truncation risk: the only available EDINET filing (period 16, body truncated) means period-16 DPU, exact LTV, and current occupancy are not directly confirmed; distribution yield classification is Category B rather than A.
- Currency basis in beta: the JPY/GBP cross is sensitive to BoJ/BoE policy divergence; computed beta of 0.39 and derived alpha of 5.7% may overstate or understate true systematic risk depending on FX regime.
