## Thesis

The sponsor, GLP Group, maintains a deep global and Japan-specific development pipeline with demonstrated willingness to recycle capital on unitholders' behalf — evidenced by the accretive GLP Tokai disposal and revised upward distribution guidance for the August 2026 period. Unit buybacks (¥5.98B completed, units cancelled) signal management's view that units trade below intrinsic value and provide technical price support. A CAPM alpha of +7.6% against a negative-returning IASP.L benchmark (currency caveat applies) reinforces the relative attraction of the 6.2% total return estimate, while the OU Monte Carlo PGain of 71.6% supports an above-average conviction score of 4 at a 12-month horizon.

## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation acceleration: further hikes beyond current 0.3% trajectory could expand cap rates and compress J-REIT NAV and distribution yield spreads more severely than the base case models.
- JPY/GBP currency noise embedded in beta calculation (Category B caveat): beta of 0.615 versus IASP.L may overstate or understate true property-market sensitivity depending on JPY direction.
- One-time distribution inflation: the August 2026 period DPU guidance revision includes asset disposal gains (GLP Tokai); recurring AFFO coverage needs monitoring as these gains are non-recurring.
- Logistics supply pipeline risk: significant new Grade-A logistics floor space under construction in Japan could push vacancy rates higher and slow rental escalation in 2027-2028.
- Unit buyback exhaustion: the board-authorised ¥13B programme is approximately 46% utilised; if remaining capacity is not deployed, a key technical price support is removed.
