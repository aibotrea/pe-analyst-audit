## Thesis

A one-step sponsor quality reduction (CRE is a mid-tier rather than tier-1 sponsor) tempers conviction to Moderate, with the JPY 30bn shelf filing indicating near-term acquisition capacity that could prove accretive if executed at appropriate cap rates.

## Key Risks
- Bank of Japan policy normalisation: further rate hikes compress J-REIT yield spreads and trigger P/NAV de-rating, particularly acute for smaller REITs facing higher refinancing costs on the JPY 30bn shelf.
- Sponsor pipeline depth: CRE Co., Ltd. is a mid-tier logistics developer; development pipeline may be insufficient to sustain growth if construction activity slows or CRE's balance sheet constrains deal flow.
- Leverage and refinancing risk: the JPY 30bn shelf filing signals potential bond issuance; if credit spreads widen or BoJ policy causes short-end rate spikes, financing costs could compress distributable income materially.
- Distribution coverage uncertainty: Period 20 (Jan–Jun 2026) filing body was truncated, preventing direct verification of AFFO coverage; if actual payout exceeds AFFO, future distributions may be at risk.
- Asset concentration: portfolio is concentrated in Greater Tokyo logistics; any major tenant vacancy or stalling of logistics rental growth could disproportionately impact income.
