## Thesis
LaSalle Logiport REIT (3466.T) offers direct exposure to large-scale, Grade-A logistics facilities anchored in the Greater Tokyo and Osaka Bay corridors, the most structurally resilient sub-market in Japanese real estate. A distribution yield of approximately 3.3% carries meaningful spread over JGB yields in an environment where BoJ normalisation appears to be plateauing, while 2.5% forward DPU growth is supported by tight vacancy, positive rent reversion, and a disciplined LaSalle-managed acquisition pipeline. Beta of 0.44 versus IASP.L (currency-basis caveat applies) indicates materially lower market sensitivity than the broader APAC REIT index, with the OU Monte Carlo producing a PGain of 74% and CAPM alpha of 5.6% supporting above-average conviction. The ongoing ¥11.8B unit buyback programme (92.5% complete as of July 2026) further evidences management capital discipline and provides incremental per-unit DPU accretion.

## Key Risks
- BoJ rate overshoot: faster-than-expected policy rate increases could widen Japanese logistics cap rates and compress asset valuations, pressuring both DPU yield spread and NAV
- New supply risk: a pipeline of large-format logistics facilities in Greater Tokyo and Osaka may push vacancy higher in secondary locations within LaSalle Logiport's portfolio, dampening rent reversion assumptions
- Currency basis in CAPM inputs: IASP.L is GBP-denominated, so computed beta and alpha embed JPY/GBP FX noise, reducing reliability of the CAPM alpha signal as a standalone measure
- Sponsor pipeline dependency: LaSalle Investment Management lacks a domestic developer relationship comparable to GLP or Prologis-affiliated J-REITs; external pipeline availability is less certain and may constrain accretive growth
- Macro data gap: Japan central bank policy rate was unavailable from stored APAC rates as of the analysis date; the risk-free rate used is the US T-bill (3.72%), which may overstate the cost of capital relative to the JGB curve (~2.67% 10Y)
