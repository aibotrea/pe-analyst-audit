## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation acceleration: further policy rate hikes and JGB yield rises would compress the logistics REIT yield spread, driving multiple contraction beyond the base case -0.5% assumption
- Logistics supply oversupply: large-scale speculative logistics developments in Greater Tokyo and Osaka completing in 2026–2028 could weaken absorption and drive vacancy higher, pressuring DPU
- JPY volatility: yen strength would reduce unhedged foreign investor demand for Japan REITs; yen weakness increases refinancing cost on any foreign-currency borrowings
- Tenant concentration risk: logistics REIT with a smaller portfolio relative to peers (GLP J-REIT, Nippon Prologis) means top-3 tenant concentration is higher, creating idiosyncratic DPU risk if a major tenant vacates
- Manager reorganisation execution risk: the newly established Management Planning Office (effective July 2026) introduces transition uncertainty; if strategic plan execution is delayed, AUM growth and accretive acquisitions may not materialise within the 12-month horizon
