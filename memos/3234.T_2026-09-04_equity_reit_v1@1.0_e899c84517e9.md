## Thesis
Mori Hills REIT Investment Corporation offers concentrated exposure to prime central Tokyo office and mixed-use assets anchored in the Roppongi Hills and ARK Hills complexes, sponsored by Mori Building Co., Ltd. — one of Japan's most prestigious urban developers. A distribution yield of approximately 5.5% at the current price of JPY 126,600, underpinned by stable semi-annual rental revenues of ~JPY 9,838–9,865M (Periods 33–34), provides a meaningful income base. A board-authorised unit buyback of JPY 1,000,000,000 (6,800 units) executed between March and July 2026 is NAV/unit accretive and signals management conviction in intrinsic value. The primary risk is BoJ policy normalisation gradually widening cap rates for prime Tokyo office, which constrains multiple expansion; asset concentration in top-3 Mori Building properties (>80% of AUM) reduces diversification, warranting a one-step qualitative gate reduction to a moderate conviction score of 3.

## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation cycle: further policy rate increases could widen cap rates for prime Tokyo office properties, compressing NAV and unit price multiples beyond the -0.5% multiple-change assumption
- Sponsor concentration: over 80% of portfolio by value is tied to Roppongi Hills/ARK Hills, meaning any deterioration in the Mori Building ecosystem (vacancy, lease non-renewal, sponsor credit) disproportionately impacts distributions
- JPY/GBP currency basis: beta and CAPM alpha are computed against GBP-denominated IASP.L; the coefficient absorbs FX co-movement, reducing precision of risk-adjusted return estimates
- Tenant non-renewal risk in large-floor-plate office: major tenants occupying flagship floors at Roppongi Hills Mori Tower could relocate as work-from-home policies evolve, with limited alternative tenant pool for super-prime floorplates
- Refinancing cost pressure: as floating-rate debt is repriced at higher BoJ-influenced short-term rates, distributable income per unit could compress even with stable gross revenues

## Invalidation Condition
Exit signal triggered if: (1) occupancy falls below 93% for two consecutive semi-annual periods, indicating meaningful tenant departure from flagship Roppongi Hills or ARK Hills assets; or (2) annualised DPU falls more than 10% below the current implied level of JPY 6,960/unit (i.e., below ~JPY 6,260) due to income shortfall or leverage-driven cost increase; or (3) BoJ policy rate exceeds 1.5%, driving a material reassessment of J-REIT cap rates; or (4) Mori Building publicly reduces its sponsor support or pipeline commitment to the REIT.
