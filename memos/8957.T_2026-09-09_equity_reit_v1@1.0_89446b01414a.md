## Key Risks
- Bank of Japan rate normalisation risk: faster-than-expected BoJ hikes would expand cap rates, compress NAV, and reduce the spread between distribution yield and risk-free alternatives (JGBs), the primary valuation anchor for J-REITs
- JPY appreciation risk: a sharp JPY strengthening could dampen inbound tourism, reducing retail tenant revenues and occupancy along the Tokyu corridor, the core driver of rental income
- Sponsor pipeline concentration: Tokyu REIT's growth depends materially on Tokyu Fudosan Holdings' willingness and capacity to inject assets; any weakening of sponsor balance sheet or strategic pivot away from REIT recycling could suppress DPU growth
- Retail sector structural risk: secular shift to e-commerce and changing consumer patterns could impair long-term rental demand for suburban Tokyo retail facilities, particularly if post-pandemic normalisation stalls
