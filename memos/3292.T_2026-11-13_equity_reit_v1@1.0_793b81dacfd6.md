## Key Risks
- BoJ policy normalisation and rising JGB yields compress the yield spread and J-REIT multiples; the policy rate is already 1.25%.
- Tenant and sponsor concentration in the AEON group; weakness in Japanese consumer retail or in the sponsor would hit rent and DPU.
- Data gaps: DPU, LTV, AFFO coverage and tenant figures could not be extracted from the truncated EDINET bodies, so the yield and gate judgements are partly unverified.
- Alpha relies on a negative trailing 5-year IASP.L return (-4.3%) and a beta that absorbs JPY/GBP currency noise (correlation only 0.49); it overstates the true edge.
- Expected return (4.5%) is barely above the USD 3-month T-bill (4.14%), and the PGain of about 69% leaves little margin for error.

## Invalidation Condition
