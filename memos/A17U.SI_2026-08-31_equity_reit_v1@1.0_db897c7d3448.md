## Key Risks
- Higher-for-longer SGD rates compressing the yield spread versus T-bills, reducing the attractiveness of the ~224bps spread relative to risk-free alternatives.
- DPU per-unit dilution persisting from the recent equity fund raise (EFR), with H1 2026 DPU up only 0.1% YoY despite distributable income growing 8.6%, until acquired assets are fully income-generating.
- Industrial and logistics vacancy risk from tech-sector demand softening or new supply additions in Singapore, which could pressure occupancy below the ~93% current level.
- Global cap rate re-expansion driven by a sustained rise in global base rates, reducing the NAV and compressing the multiple at which A17U trades.
- Concentration risk from SG industrial exposure — while A17U has diversified into AU, UK, and US assets, Singapore remains the dominant geography, creating sensitivity to local regulatory and macroeconomic shifts.
