## Key Risks
- Orchard Road retail secular headwinds: e-commerce substitution and tourist-traffic dependency could compress occupancy and rental reversions at Wisma Atria, directly reducing distributable income.
- Higher-for-longer Singapore interest rates (SORA/SIBOR): Starhill carries floating-rate debt exposure; a sustained SORA above 3.5% would erode interest cover and compress the net yield spread over the risk-free rate.
- Sponsor pipeline limitation: YTL Corporation has a smaller and less liquid pipeline relative to Temasek- or CapitaLand-backed REITs, constraining AUM growth and long-term DPU compounding potential.
- AUD/SGD currency translation risk: The Australian portfolio (AUD 377.5M valuation as at 30 June 2026) introduces FX translation risk on both income and NAV; AUD depreciation versus SGD reduces distributable income.
- Distribution coverage thinness: Starhill has historically distributed close to 100% of distributable income; any one-off cost increase (debt refinancing, capex) or income shortfall could result in DPU coverage falling below 1.0x AFFO.
