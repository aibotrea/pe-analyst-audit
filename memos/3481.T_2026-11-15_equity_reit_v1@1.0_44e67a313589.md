## Key Risks
- Rising BoJ policy rate and JGB yields could widen J-REIT cap rates and compress the multiple, a risk the base case does not model.
- The guided DPU step-down (JPY 3,117 to 2,936) depends on gain-on-sale from the phased Inzai 1 disposal, so the underlying coverage and sustainable DPU level are uncertain.
- Leverage, AFFO coverage, tenant concentration and WALE could not be verified from the truncated EDINET bodies, so the qualitative gates rest on limited data and the DPU figures come from a single news summary.
- Beta and market return come from a GBP-denominated benchmark. The negative 5-year IASP.L return inflates CAPM alpha, and the USD T-bill is used as the risk-free rate for a JPY asset.
- Tokyo Bay and Kansai logistics supply additions could pressure rents and occupancy.
