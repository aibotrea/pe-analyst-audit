## Thesis

At JPY 119,000, the trailing distribution yield of approximately 3.2% offers a modest but positive spread over the current 10yr JGB yield, though the spread is compressing as the Bank of Japan normalises monetary policy from its zero-rate era. Conviction is moderate at 3/5, reflecting the stable asset quality and sponsor strength offset by genuine headwinds from BoJ rate normalisation, cap-rate expansion risk, and limited DPU growth visibility in a mature office market.
