8985.T is a pure-play Japan hotel REIT with leveraged exposure to inbound tourism, ADR growth and variable-rent upside. The 2026 equity raise lifted units from 5.10m to 5.90m and total assets to JPY 688.8bn, which supports growth if the acquisitions are accretive. An estimated forward yield near 4.8% plus modest growth gives E(R) of 5.8%, a 12-month OU Monte Carlo PGain of about 68%, and CAPM alpha of 6.4% (inflated by the negative benchmark return). Beta of 0.56 against IASP.L carries the currency-basis caveat. The price is about 1.5x book after a recent rally, and the yield is a derived estimate, so conviction is moderate rather than high.

## Key Risks
- Hotel earnings are cyclical and tied to inbound tourism, so FX moves, geopolitical events or a travel shock could cut RevPAR and variable rent quickly.
- The DPU and yield are derived estimates because the filing body was truncated before the published distribution table. Actual DPU, AFFO coverage and LTV were not verified.
- Rising JGB yields and BoJ tightening would raise refinancing costs and could expand cap rates, which would hurt a REIT with balance-sheet liabilities near 50% of assets.
- The expected market return from IASP.L is negative over 5 years, which flatters CAPM alpha. Beta uses a GBP benchmark, so the currency basis adds noise. Rf is a USD T-bill rate and not a JPY rate.
- Dilution and integration risk after the 2026 equity raise, plus a price-to-book premium of about 1.5x, leave limited valuation cushion.

## Invalidation Condition
Exit if the next semi-annual DPU is cut materially below the roughly JPY 4,100 annualised run-rate assumed here, or if DPU coverage by distributable income falls below 1.0x. Also exit if reported LTV rises above 55% or refinancing spreads widen sharply. A sustained fall in hotel RevPAR or occupancy for two consecutive reporting periods, driven by weaker inbound tourism, would also invalidate the thesis.
