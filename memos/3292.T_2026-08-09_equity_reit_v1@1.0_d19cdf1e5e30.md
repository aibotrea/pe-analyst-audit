## Thesis
AEON REIT Investment Corporation (3292.T) offers stable JPY-denominated income backed by long-term master leases with AEON Group, Japan's largest retailer, providing high predictability of cash flows across its portfolio of large-format shopping centres. A trailing DPU yield of approximately 4.25% delivers a positive spread over current JPY risk-free rates, though the spread has compressed as the Bank of Japan continues its rate normalisation path. Beta of 0.46 versus IASP.L (currency-basis caveat applies) indicates below-benchmark volatility, and the OU Monte Carlo PGain of 73.4% supports a moderate conviction at a 12-month horizon. The key risk is BoJ policy acceleration compressing J-REIT valuations further, combined with AEON Group's concentrated tenant exposure, which constrains the conviction ceiling to a moderate 3 despite positive CAPM alpha of 5.0%.
