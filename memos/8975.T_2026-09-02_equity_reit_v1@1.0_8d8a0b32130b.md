## Key Risks
- Bank of Japan rate normalisation: further policy rate increases would raise JGB yields, expand cap rates, and directly compress J-REIT distributions and unit prices — Ichigo's ~46% LTV exposes it to refinancing cost increases.
- Hybrid-work structural demand risk: sustained remote/flexible working adoption by Japanese corporates could weigh on mid-size office lease renewal rates and push vacancy above the 7–8% threshold at which DPU coverage becomes strained.
- Sponsor pipeline depth: Ichigo Group Holdings (TSE: 2337) is a mid-tier listed sponsor; its pipeline of acquisitions and ability to inject assets at accretive cap rates is more limited than major J-REIT sponsors (Mitsui Fudosan, Mitsubishi Estate), constraining AUM growth.
- JPY currency volatility: For non-JPY investors, JPY weakness erodes USD/EUR total returns; conversely JPY strength could attract foreign inflows but has historically been accompanied by global risk-off events unfavourable to REIT multiples.
