- Tokyo office demand structural headwind: hybrid and remote work adoption by Japanese corporates may reduce medium-term office absorption, increasing vacancy rates and suppressing rent growth beyond the base case assumption
- JPY/GBP FX basis in beta: beta of 0.615 computed against the GBP-denominated IASP.L benchmark absorbs currency co-movement noise, making CAPM alpha (Category B) an unreliable standalone signal

- Unit split execution risk: while the announced unit split should improve liquidity, mispricing during the split implementation window may create short-term price volatility
