## Key Risks
- BoJ accelerated rate normalisation: further JGB yield increases could compress J-REIT yield spreads and trigger sector multiple contraction, the most material downside risk for the 12-month horizon
- JPY/GBP FX basis in beta: computed beta of 0.60 absorbs currency co-movement, creating noise in the CAPM alpha signal and complicating hedge decisions for non-JPY investors
