## Key Risks
- Bank of Japan rate normalisation: faster-than-expected policy rate increases (above 1.0%) would compress J-REIT spreads, widen cap rates, and pressure floating-rate debt costs, directly reducing distributable income.
- Tokyo office demand softness: a deterioration in Grade-A office leasing fundamentals driven by hybrid working trends or a domestic economic slowdown could push occupancy below 92%, eroding rental income.
- JPY/GBP currency-basis noise in beta and alpha: the IASP.L benchmark is GBP-denominated; computed beta of 0.53 and CAPM alpha of 6.5% absorb bilateral FX co-movement and may overstate the precision of these signals.
- Refinancing and debt cost risk: KDX Realty's investment corporation bond programme (up to JPY 10bn) and ongoing borrowings face refinancing risk in a rising BoJ rate environment; the absence of live BoJ rate data at as_of adds estimation uncertainty.
- Buyback programme completion and capital allocation: the self-buyback consuming ~JPY 6bn of capital by end-September 2026 limits near-term acquisition capacity; if market conditions deteriorate post-buyback, NAV-accretive acquisitions may be constrained.
