## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation: further policy rate increases compress J-REIT valuations via cap rate expansion and higher refinancing costs on the fund's floating-rate debt; this is the dominant macro risk for the position.
- JPY/GBP currency-basis noise in beta estimate inflates or deflates CAPM alpha; the direction of the noise is not hedged in this analysis.
- Related-party transaction disclosure (Sep 2026 filing) indicates the sponsor-related party threshold was breached; while not negative per se, it signals elevated acquisition activity that could be dilutive if assets are acquired at sub-optimal yields.
- Residential lease WALE is structurally short (~2 years for Japanese residential), creating re-leasing risk if rent growth decelerates or vacancy rises in key Tokyo submarkets.
- Filing body was truncated (full 273-page annual report not fully parsed); exact LTV, AFFO coverage, and lease-expiry profile could not be independently verified — a data-completeness risk in this analysis.
