## Key Risks
- GBP/SGD currency depreciation eroding SGD-denominated DPU — Elite UK REIT's entire asset and income base is GBP-denominated, creating direct translation risk for Singapore unitholders.
- UK government tenant concentration risk: >90% dependence on DWP and related government departments; any structural change to UK public sector property strategy (hotdesking, office consolidation, lease non-renewal) would materially impair income.
- Bank of England rate policy and UK commercial property cap rate expansion compressing NAV; UK office sector re-rating risk remains elevated given hybrid-working structural demand shifts.
- Aggregate leverage unconfirmed from first-party filings (no stored filings retrieved); if gearing is approaching the 45% MAS limit, refinancing risk in a higher-for-longer GBP rate environment could pressure distributions.
- Smaller sponsor with limited capital injection capacity versus institutional peers; pipeline of accretive acquisitions may be constrained, limiting growth beyond organic lease indexation.
