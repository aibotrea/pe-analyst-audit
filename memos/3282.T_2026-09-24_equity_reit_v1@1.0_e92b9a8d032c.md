## Key Risks
- BoJ rate normalisation accelerating beyond current market expectations, widening the cap rate spread and compressing residential REIT valuations across Japan.
- Post-offering unit dilution reducing DPU per unit if newly acquired assets underperform the stated acquisition yield, particularly in a softening Tokyo rental market.
- JPY/GBP FX volatility introducing noise into the IASP.L-benchmarked beta and alpha estimates, making CAPM-derived signals less reliable for this JPY-denominated instrument.
- Tenant concentration risk in urban Tokyo submarkets: any sharp increase in vacancy from competing supply or demographic shifts could reduce distributable income.
- Regulatory and tax changes affecting J-REIT distribution requirements or real estate transaction taxes, which could alter the effective payout ratio.
