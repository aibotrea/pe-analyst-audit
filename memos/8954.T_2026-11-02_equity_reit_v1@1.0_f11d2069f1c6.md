## Thesis
ORIX JREIT is a diversified Japanese REIT sponsored by ORIX, yielding about 5.4% on recurring distributions at a price of JPY 86,500. Reported H2 FY2026 DPU of about JPY 2,504 beat guidance only because of property-sale gains and rent hikes, and management reportedly guides to a DPU cut next fiscal year. The OU Monte Carlo gives a 12-month expected return of about 3.5% with a standard deviation of about 10.7%. That implies PGain of about 62.7%, and the E(R) sits slightly below the 3.99% T-bill rate. Positive CAPM alpha of 4.5% is mainly an artefact of a negative 5-year IASP.L return and a beta of 0.57 that carries currency-basis noise. Because of the guided cut and weak price momentum, the distribution-coverage gate applies a one-step downgrade to conviction 2.
