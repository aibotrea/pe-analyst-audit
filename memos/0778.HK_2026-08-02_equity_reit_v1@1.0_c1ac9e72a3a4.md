- Interest rate sensitivity: HKD is pegged to USD via currency board; HIBOR closely tracks US rates, meaning higher-for-longer US rates compress the yield spread and elevate refinancing costs on floating-rate debt.
- ESR Group corporate risk: parent ESR Group has pivoted toward logistics and data centres; potential changes to manager fee structures, dilutive capital markets activity, or a discounted privatisation attempt could impair unitholder returns.
- HK residential property market decline: sustained weakness in HK residential prices could reduce housing-estate retail footfall, impairing occupancy and DPU coverage assumptions.
- Filing body truncation: FY2025 DPU and AFFO coverage ratio could not be fully verified from the truncated 2025 Annual Report filing body (0778.HK 2026-04-24); distribution yield assumption carries elevated Category B uncertainty.
