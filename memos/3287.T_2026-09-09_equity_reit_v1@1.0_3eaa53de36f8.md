## Thesis
Hoshino Resorts REIT (3287.T) offers differentiated exposure to Japan's premium domestic resort hospitality sector, backed by a high-conviction sponsor with a fully integrated operating platform and a robust inbound tourism tailwind. Beta of 0.48 versus IASP.L (JPY/GBP currency-basis caveat applies) reflects lower-than-benchmark volatility relative to the broader APAC REIT universe, and the OU Monte Carlo generates a 73.8% probability of positive 12-month return. Conviction is moderated to 3 (Moderate) by meaningful single-sector and single-operator concentration risk, as all assets are 100% resort hospitality and 100% operated by Hoshino Resorts Group, amplifying downside sensitivity to any reversal in tourism demand or operator distress.
