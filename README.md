# PE Analyst — Portfolio Audit Trail

This repository is the public, dated record of PE Analyst, a personal research
project that tests an AI-driven investment process on listed Asia-Pacific REITs.
It holds the method (`METHODOLOGY.md`), the research memos (`memos/`), every
decision on two simulated portfolios (`portfolio_a/`, `portfolio_b/`) and the
quarterly results (`outcomes/`).

## Important information

**This notice applies to every file in this repository, including files
committed before it was added on 22 November 2026.**

PE Analyst is a personal research project. Both portfolios are simulated: no
money is invested and no orders are placed. Returns are calculated from market
closing prices under the assumptions set out in `METHODOLOGY.md`, and have the
limitations of any simulated record.

Nothing in this repository is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer
or solicitation to buy or sell any security. It takes no account of any
reader's objectives or circumstances. A conviction score, target weight,
"accept", "reject", "hold" or "dispose" in a memo or decision file is a record
of what a simulated portfolio did under the published method. It is not a view
that any reader should act on. Past and simulated performance is not a guide to
future results.

Memos, decisions and analysis are produced in whole or in part by AI language
models. They can contain errors, including errors of fact about the companies
named, and they are published as written and not corrected afterwards. Figures
marked Category B or C in a memo are estimates or assumptions, not reported
data. Do not rely on any file here as a source of information about a company;
use the company's own filings.

The views are the author's own and not those of any employer or other
organisation.

At 22 November 2026 the author holds no position in any security named in
this repository. The author may take positions in future without notice. The
simulated portfolios are not a record of any personal holding.

## How the record is kept

- Files are published when a decision is made and are not edited afterwards.
  Errors are corrected by a new commit, and history is never rewritten.
- Changes to the method are dated in `METHODOLOGY.md` and
  `platform_changes.json`.
- Memo and decision files published from 22 November 2026 carry a short form
  of this notice. Earlier files do not, because published files are not
  edited; this notice covers them.
